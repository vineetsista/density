# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that builds a good project:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback. This project's whole
  premise is that a measured number beats an assertion, so "here is what I
  measured" is always a welcome disagreement.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainer at vineet.sista@gmail.com. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainer will
respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's leadership.
